# Exercise images

Source: [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), published as an open public-domain exercise dataset under the Unlicense (see LICENSE.txt). Downloaded 8 November 2026. Photos are unchanged.

The mapping, original exercise names and individual source URLs are recorded in exercise-images.js. Two photos per exercise show the source positions. Related movements are explicitly labelled in the UI and should not be interpreted as exact demonstrations of the named workout.
